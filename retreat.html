Recreational Therapy Programs

Healing Through Recreational Therapy

Our comprehensive recreational therapy programs connect veterans and first responders with diverse healing experiences. From hiking mountain trails and hunting expeditions to equine therapy and working with therapy animals, each activity is carefully designed to promote healing and personal growth. We also offer unique water-based therapies including scuba diving, surfing, and fishing - all guided by experienced professionals who understand the journey to recovery.
